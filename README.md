## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Fell & Sell**. This trainer enhances the core Fell & Sell gameplay loop by providing control over survival, dungeon rewards, crafting resources, shop finances, and progression. It is designed for players who want to explore deeper dungeons, experiment with equipment and altars, expand their shop, or reduce the risk of losing an unbanked haul.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for single-player users who want to experiment with builds, protect valuable expedition loot, speed up crafting, expand their shop more freely, or reduce repetitive resource management.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore the forest and descend through the dungeon from the area behind your shop. Movement and positioning are important during expeditions, especially when avoiding groups of skeletons, monstrous enemies, goblin raids, storms, and other hazards. Use the trainer's survival options to focus on discovering routes, collecting resources, and reaching deeper areas.

### Tools & Inventory

Loot gathered during an expedition remains unbanked until you return safely. Normally, dying causes the entire unbanked haul to be lost. Use haul protection and resource options when testing equipment, collecting rare drops, or managing large quantities of materials. Craft weapons, armor, furniture, and other useful items at the cabin before starting the next run.

### World & Environment

Fell & Sell combines dungeon crawling with forest foraging and shop development. Dungeon altars can significantly change the player's build, while forest events create unpredictable exploration conditions. Quest Board objectives can be exchanged for gold, gear, and items. Shop decorations also provide permanent combat bonuses and can improve sales appeal.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is built around first-person mod-and-slash encounters against skeletons and other monsters. Stronger weapons and armor allow deeper dungeon runs, while altar effects can alter the player's size, damage potential, mobility, and combat behavior. Activate health protection or environmental resistance before difficult encounters, and disable options when you want the intended risk-and-reward experience.
